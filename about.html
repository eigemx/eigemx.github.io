Hi! My name is Mohamed, and I'm a mechanical engineer with a passion for scientific computing. I'm particularly interested in computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and OpenFOAM. Here are some of the projects I'm proud of:

  • neatmesh: A mesh quality inspector written in Python.
  • unvpp: A C++17 library for reading the Ideas-UNV mesh format.
